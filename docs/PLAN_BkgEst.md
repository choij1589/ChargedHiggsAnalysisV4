# PLAN: nonprompt-lepton background for 2024, fake rates and closure (phases 3.1-3.3)

Goal: measure the tight-to-loose (fake) rates of muons and electrons in 2024 data and simulation, define their uncertainty from variations of the measurement (not from the closure), and test the method with an MC closure whose pass/fail criterion is fixed before it is run. Scope of this round (user, 2026-11-25): fake-rate measurement and closure only. Prompt-rate treatment (3.2) enters only as far as the closure needs it; conversions (3.4), WZ (3.5) and normalization sources (3.6) follow later, after the lepton and trigger SFs and the trilepton skims.

References: B2G-25-013 (AN-25-154 `Sections/BkgEst.tex` 6.1; old SKNano `Analyzers/src/MeasFakeRateV4.cc`, `ClosFakeRate.cc`, `TriLeptonBase.cc:75-107`; V3 `MeasFakeRateV4/python/`, `docs/CLOSURETEST.md`); CWR review of B2G-25-013, `~/Sync/Documents/Paper/B2G-25-013/Reviews/CWR/forNextIteration.md` items 6-8 and `comments/COMMENTS_06_BKGEST.md`, `COMMENTS_08_SYSTUNC.md`. ## 2. StatComm comment and what it changes

R07-010 (Pietro for the StatComm): a closure test is pass/fail; taking the non-closure as the systematic uncertainty is statistically wrong. B2G-25-013 kept its treatment (option A). forNextIteration 7 proposes to reduce the QCD -> tt transfer effect directly and keep the closure as a test; item 8 (Greg, R06-013) asks to measure eps_p or quantify eps_p < 1 separately, which the old closure absorbed. Consequences here:

- The rate uncertainty is built from the measurement: statistics of the rates; prompt-subtraction normalization; away-jet pT (energy scale of the source); b-tag requirement or flavour split (source type). Each variation is a full set of rates, applied to the application region, so its effect on every SR template bin is known (shape nuisances or lnN, F1).
- The method is changed to remove the transfer effect at the source (F2), before the closure is looked at.
- The closure is run once with the final method and the criterion of F3. If it fails, the method is revised (F2 options in order), not the uncertainty. Outcome 2026-11-27: mu mu mu passes, e mu mu fails (A) at the margin (1.07 sigma); kept for the feasibility study (user), the prompt subtraction method is an open item for the next revision round, after the full limit calculation.

## 3. Design

- **Analyzer `MeasFakeRate`** (new, `HcToWA_Analyzers`, MeasTrigEff pattern). Efficiency fixes against V4, where the time went (`LOG_BkgEst.md` 2026-11-25):
  1. trigger decision first (V4 built jets and muons and ran the noise filter and veto map before it; most data events fail the prescaled paths);
  2. one object pass: loose leptons with a tight flag (V4 ran loose and tight as two full passes);
  3. selection variations (away-jet pT 20/30/40/60 GeV, b-tag requirement) as cuts on cached quantities (leading away-jet pT, number of b jets; jets selected once at 20 GeV), not object re-selections (V4: `evtLoopAgain` per variation);
  4. pre-booked histogram handles: one 2D (\|eta\|, pTcorr) per (region QCD/W/Z, trigger, loose/tight, variation, MC flavour of the mother jet) plus a few central 1D control histograms (MT, MET, m(ll), nPV, lepton pT/eta); V4 filled 1-2k string-keyed histograms per event (~10^4 fine-binned histograms per job, `std::map<string>` lookup per fill), of which only bin integrals are used;
  5. object systematics (JES, JER, lepton scales, unclustered MET) only in the Z-region normalization, or replaced by the flat prompt-normalization variation (F5).
  Modes by user flag: `MeasMuFR` (Muon0/1), `MeasElFR` (EGamma0/1); MC runs both.
- **Skim (F6):** `Skim_FakeRate`, one prescaled fake-rate path fired and >= 1 lepton candidate (NanoAOD pT > 8 GeV, no ID), on the data PDs and the QCD-enriched MC, to /pnfs SKNanoSkim; the prompt MC (W, DY, tt, ST, VV) runs unskimmed (the skim keeps ~80% of it). Reruns for method changes then take minutes.
- **Rates script** (`MeasFakeRate/python/`, NanoV15 locally and on KNU): prompt scales per (trigger, ID) from the Z region; rates per bin with the prompt subtraction and stat errors (data: binomial on the subtracted counts with MC stat; MC: weighted binomial); variations; correctionlib JSON (`data`, per variation; `MC_QCD`, `MC_TT`, per flavour) in `HcToWA_Analyzers/data/2024/FakeRate/`. Plots with `Common/Tools/plotter.py`.
- **Closure analyzer `ClosFakeRate`** (port): runs on the Skim_TriLep TT skims (TTLL, TTLJ powheg); SR-like eμμ and μμμ selections as B2G-25-013; observed = all-tight events; predicted = application region with the fake weights; also the generator-matched split (loose-not-tight leptons nonprompt vs prompt) to separate the transfer effect from the eps_p = 1 effect (F4). Needs the Skim_TriLep production (planned after the measurements).
- **Checks:** prompt normalization in the W-enriched region (MT > 60 GeV) as an independent test of the Z-region scale; nPV of prescaled-trigger data vs MC in the Z region (prescale columns change with luminosity, so the PU profile of these paths can differ from the full-luminosity one; reweight per trigger if needed); QCD vs tt MC rates per flavour (the size of the transfer effect in 2024).

## 5. Open decisions (user)

- **F1 (decided, user 2026-11-27):** one shape nuisance per lepton flavour (`CMS_..._nonprompt_mu`, `CMS_..._nonprompt_el`), shared by the e mu mu and mu mu mu channels: up/down nonprompt templates from the rates central +- the bin-by-bin total uncertainty (stat (+) PromptNorm (+) AwayJet (+) BTag; JSON syst `Total_Up` / `Total_Down`), coherent over the rate bins. Differs from B2G-25-013 (lnN per era and channel from the closure chi2 scan): the uncertainty follows the lepton flavour, not the channel. The application-region statistics are in the templates.
- **F2 (decided, user 2026-11-27):** no transfer correction in the feasibility study; data rates with the bin-by-bin uncertainty (b-tag and away-jet variations cover the source dependence). The flavour split of QCD and tt MC is kept as information (`MeasFakeRate/plots/AN/2024/FakeRate/flavour_*`).
- **F3 (decided, user 2026-11-27):** MC closure in tt (TTLL + TTLJ powheg Skim_TriLep skims) per channel (e mu mu, mu mu mu), with the rates measured in QCD MC (`FakeRate_<flavour>`, syst `MC_QCD`). Pass only if both hold, in each channel: (A) yield: |N_obs / N_pred - 1| < sigma_total, sigma_total = MC statistics of observed and predicted (+) the rate uncertainty (each variation of the data rate, relative, applied to the prediction: Stat, PromptNorm, AwayJet, BTag); (B) shape: chi2 of observed vs predicted in the fit variable (m(mumu) for e mu mu; both pair masses for mu mu mu), bins merged to n_eff >= 10, covariance = MC statistics per bin + each rate variation fully correlated over bins; p > 0.05. Diagnostics, not pass/fail: on-Z / off-Z (mu mu mu), the generator-matched split of the loose-not-tight leptons (nonprompt / prompt, F4), and the prediction with the data rates.
- **F4 (decided, user 2026-11-27): option (a).** eps_p = 1 in the weight; its effect is quantified by the generator-matched split of the closure (prompt loose-not-tight leptons in the tt application region) (the prompt loose-to-tight efficiency from TnP is not measured in the feasibility study, user 2026-11-27). Move to the full matrix method only if the effect is not small against the F1 uncertainty.
- **F5 (decided, user 2026-11-25; no floor; trigger-object matching dropped 2026-11-26, see F9):** prompt scale s per (path, ID) = N_data / N_MC in a Z region: path fired; exactly two opposite-sign leptons of the measured flavour, both passing the ID being normalized, no other veto lepton; leading lepton above the path's offline pT cut; away jet as in the measurement region; \|m(ll) - 91.2\| < 15 GeV; MC = DY + tt + VV + W (+ ST); non-Z content checked with same-sign pairs and sidebands; nPV per path checked first (per-path PU reweighting if needed). Uncertainty delta per (path, flavour) = (a) Z-window statistics (+) (b) W-region transfer \|N_data / (s N_MC) - 1\| (single tight lepton, away jet, MT > 60 GeV; inclusive and per pT) (+) (c) object systematics on the transfer ratio R = N_MC(MET < 25, MT < 25) / N_MC(Z window) in the prompt MC (JES, JER, unclustered, lepton scales; up/down maximum per source; MET, MT and away-jet pT recomputed with the leptons fixed; prompt MC jobs only). **No 15% floor**; the measured delta is reported next to B2G-25-013's 15%. Variation: s -> s (1 +- delta), rates recomputed; one nuisance per flavour, coherent over paths and bins. The prompt fraction s T_MC / T_data is reported per bin (input to F7).
- **F6 (decided, user 2026-11-25):** skim: `Skim_FakeRate` (one prescaled fake-rate path fired and >= 1 lepton candidate) on the data PDs and the QCD MC only (user: prompt MC unskimmed), once, written to /pnfs SKNanoSkim (write exception of the user, 2026-11-24); the measurement runs on the skims.
- **F7 (decided, user 2026-11-27):** V4 pT bins; electron |eta_SC| edges {0, 0.8, 1.444, 2.5} (the ID's barrel/endcap boundary) instead of 1.479; the highest pT bins are not merged; a pT bin whose rate goes negative after the prompt subtraction takes the rate of the previous (lower) pT bin of the same |eta| row (user 2026-11-27).
- **F9 (decided, user 2026-11-25; revised 2026-11-26):** no trigger-object matching anywhere: not in the fake-rate measurement (user 2026-11-26, same treatment for muons and electrons, since NanoAODv15 stores no trigger object for the Mu8/Mu17_TrkIsoVVL legs: no quality bit covers their final filters, and objects without a bit are dropped; LOG_BkgEst 2026-11-25), and not in the analysis selections (SR, application region, control regions, closure). Offline pT cuts above the online thresholds, as V4.
- **F8 (decided, user 2026-11-27):** for electrons, EMEnriched and bcToE combined as in V4; muons: MuEnriched only. Checked 2026-11-27 (LOG_BkgEst): the 2024 EMEnriched fragments veto the bcToE filter (`~bctoefilter`), so the two samples are disjoint and simply add.
